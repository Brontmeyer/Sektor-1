// =====================================
// STATUS EFFECTS
// =====================================

- [ ] Poison - Saps a small amount of HP periodically during battle.
- [ ] Sleep - Renders the unit unable to act until worn off or hit.
- [ ] Silence - Prevents the use of magic, summons, and enemy skills.
- [ ] Darkness - Reduces physical attack accuracy; magic is unaffected.
- [ ] Confusion - Causes the character to randomly attack allies and enemies.
- [ ] Berserk - Increases strength and forces continuous attacks, but lowers accuracy and makes the character uncontrollable.
- [ ] Slow - Slows the filling of the ATB gauge, increasing turn wait times.
- [ ] Stop - Completely freezes the ATB gauge for a duration.
- [ ] Paralyzed - Temporarily stops a unit from taking actions.
- [ ] Petrify - Turns the character to stone, removing them from combat (results in a game over if the whole party is affected).
- [ ] Frog / Small - Reduces physical damage to 1 and restricts magic use (Frog limits to Toad spells).
- [ ] Death-sentence - Places a timer overhead, leading to instant KO when it hits zero.
- [ ] Death - Lying motionless in battle; loses the game if all active allies fall.

- [ ] Haste - Speeds up the rate at which the ATB gauge fills.
- [ ] Regen - Restores HP gradually over time.
- [ ] Barrier - Halves physical damage taken.
- [ ] MBarrier - Halves magic damage taken.
- [ ] Reflect - Bounces targeted spells back at the caster.
- [ ] Shield - Grants comprehensive protection against most damage/status applications.
- [ ] Fury - Increases damage dealt while making surge gauges fill faster (halved accuracy or higher intake).
- [ ] Sadness - Reduces damage taken by half, but slows down limit gauge accumulation.
- [ ] Resist - rants immunity to specific elements.
